## Primary priority: 2015 Rosh Hashanah sermon

**Work:** Rabbi Samuel M. Cohon, *The Necessary Chutzpah of Saving Refugees* (Temple Emanu-El, Tucson; 2015).

1. **Contemporaneous published evidence:** [Union for Reform Judaism's second annual Rosh Hashanah sermon roundup](https://urj.org/blog/our-second-annual-rosh-hashanah-sermon-round) names Cohon, the congregation and the sermon, publishes selected passages, and links the Temple's old full-sermon URL.
2. **New methodological evidence:** The editor explicitly says that the roundup includes only sermons for which the editor could obtain a written text, and that the portions quoted were the editor's selected extracts. This makes it reasonable to infer that a *written version of this sermon was available to that editor in 2015*. It is **not** evidence that any publisher currently preserves that manuscript, or that an original Temple-hosted version has been recovered.

**Named 2015 roundup editor:** Mark J. Pelavin, credited in the publisher page's *About the Author* section. This identifies the contemporary collector, **not** a present-day manuscript custodian. Future approach to Pelavin or URJ requires separate express authorization; none has been requested or sent.
