### Verification/identity trap
- American Jewish Archives' **Samuel S. Cohon Papers (1908–1959)** relate to Samuel *Solomon* Cohon (1888–1959), **not Rabbi Samuel M. Cohon's 2010s Temple Emanu-El sermons**. Similar names do not establish authorship. Do not ingest these archival sermons into this atlas.
