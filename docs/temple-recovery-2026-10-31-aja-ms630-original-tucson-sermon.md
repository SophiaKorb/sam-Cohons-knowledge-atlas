# November archival continuation — AJA MS-630 contains a genuine Tucson Temple sermon, but not Cohon's

**Research date:** October 31, 2026. **Branch:** `research/2026-10-21-too-jewish-intake`. **Scope:** verified public metadata and documentary provenance only.

## Significant new item-level accession: MS-630 Box 11 Folder 3 (1993)

The [Jacob Rader Marcus Center of the American Jewish Archives digital original folder](https://collections.americanjewisharchives.org/ms/ms0630/ms0630.011.003.pdf) is publicly accessible through its own source domain. The institutional wrapper identifies:

- **MS-630**, Rabbi Alexander M. Schindler Digital Collection, Series A, Union of American Hebrew Congregations, 1961–1996.
- **Box 11, Folder 3:** *Outreach [Missionizing], 1993–1994*.
- A dated sermon typescript, **“Outreach in the Synagogue: A Plan for the Future”**, **December 3, 1993**, by **Rabbi Thomas A. Louchheim** of **Temple Emanu-El, 225 North Country Club Road, Tucson, Arizona**.
- Louchheim's **December 17, 1993** letter to Rabbi Alexander M. Schindler states that he enclosed a sermon he had delivered to his congregation. Schindler's **December 29, 1993** response acknowledges the letter and sermon.

**Source verification:** The archive's original scanned-folder text includes the title, name and dates, the correct Tucson letterhead, contemporaneous correspondence, and attached sermon. Unlike a general thematic collection, this is a **specific item/folder with a specific published digital file**. The archive's PDF text was examined at lines showing the dated correspondence and sermon; a required PDF page-0 screenshot failed in the web screenshot backend with cache miss. Thus **visual QC, binary download and SHA-256 were not performed**, and no formal authenticity or copyright clearance beyond the original institutional hosting is claimed.

**The non-Cohon boundary is fundamental:** Louchheim's documented 1993 sermon predates Cohon's Tucson rabbinate, and the MS-630 collection ends in 1996. This directly demonstrates that some original Temple Emanu-El Tucson sermon correspondence was preserved by the AJA, **not** that AJA holds Cohon's 2000 Yom Kippur, delivered 2001 Rosh Hashanah or 2015 refugee sermons. It would be an attribution error to catalogue this item under Samuel M. Cohon or to count it as his recording or published work.

## Other target-specific discovery attempts

New source-domain searches were directed at the digitized AJA item pages, other public archives, original Temple title references, the first-party Hearing Voices site and PRX piece indexes. No independently named **Samuel M. Cohon** original of the three requested sermons was identified. The existing [AJA MS-674 September 11 sermon collection](https://7079.sydneyplus.com/archive/final/Theme.aspx?f=FindingAids%2Fms0674.pdf&m=resource&r=692678) remains relevant as an institutional collection, but its contributor index does not name Cohon. The **2001 delivered** version and **pre-9/11 undelivered** drafts remain separate evidence categories.

The [Hearing Voices first-party index](https://hearingvoices.com/hv-series/) still identifies **HV009, April 30, 2008**, and Cohon's presenter credit. A publisher-site sitemap and search/API routes were checked as different discovery methods but were inaccessible in the available reader; no actual **HV009** audio enclosure or PRX piece ID was found. These failures do not establish that the original media does not exist. No audio filenames were guessed from the publisher's distinct HV073/HV074/HV101 MP3 examples.

2000 *The Origins of Awareness* remains known through an attributed third-party reproduction of a multi-author course document; 2015 *The Necessary Chutzpah of Saving Refugees* remains excerpt-only at the URJ. The original 2015 HIAS signed rabbinic letter is separate **collective advocacy**, not that individually authored sermon. The 2013 Temple Podbean channel remains a historical newsletter lead without verified class GUID/enclosure.

## Archival disposition and repeat gate

- **Verified positive:** AJA MS-630 contains an original **1993 Tucson Temple Emanu-El sermon, by Thomas A. Louchheim**. It is a useful institutional archival precedent and a source-specific, independently checkable accession, but not an Atlas Cohon writing.
