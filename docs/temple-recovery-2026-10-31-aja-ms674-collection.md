# October 31, 2026 — AJA September 11 sermon collection and Tucson identity QA

## Archival discovery: AJA MS-674 is a real, unusually relevant 2001 collection

The [American Jewish Archives published finding aid](https://7079.sydneyplus.com/archive/final/Theme.aspx?f=FindingAids%2Fms0674.pdf&m=resource&r=692678) documents the **September 11 Sermon Collection, MS-674**, one Hollinger box / 0.4 linear feet, processed in February 2002. AJA director Gary P. Zola solicited sermons by Reform rabbis in response to September 11, 2001, including High Holy Day sermons delivered just afterward. The aid explicitly notes that **most collected manuscripts are drafts**, with some items previously printed in bulletins or newsletters.

This is closely matched by **theme and year** to Rabbi Samuel M. Cohon's *delivered replacement* Erev Rosh Hashanah 5762 address, which Cohon quoted retrospectively in a [2026 first-party sermon](https://beitsimchatucson.org/blog/911-25-years-before-and-since). However, **Cohon does not occur in the MS-674 finding aid's contributor name index**. The inventory lists nine surname-range folders in Box 1, with **no Cohon-specific manuscript or item identifier**. The index lists other contributors such as **Thomas A. Louchheim**, but that cannot be transferred to Cohon or used to infer any particular version, relationship or authorial custody.

**Evidence disposition:** authenticated thematic archive collection, **not authenticated Cohon manuscript custody**. Nonappearance in the finding aid also does not prove no unindexed or later archival item exists. AJA says its catalog does not describe every item and most holdings are not digitally accessible. There was **no outreach or request to AJA**. No archive-original binary or sermon text was retrieved.

**Crucial version distinction:** A 2001 draft in this collection, even if eventually confirmed as Cohon's, would still require its own evidence to determine whether it matches the **delivered replacement sermon** or one of the earlier **undelivered pre-September 11 drafts**. No version equivalence should be assumed.

### PDF inspection limitations

The finding aid PDF was accessible to the web text parser; its full listed names, scope and folder ranges were read. The mandated screenshot on page index 0 failed due to a PDF cache miss, so visual fidelity and exact PDF bytes were not verified. Do not describe the finding aid as byte-authenticated.

## Related AJA digitized bulletin collection — separate congregation

The [AJA finding aids index](https://www.americanjewisharchives.org/research/finding-aids/) also lists **Temple Bulletin Collection, MS-882**, a large multi-congregation twentieth/twenty-first-century series. At least one first-party [April 2001 digital bulletin](https://collections.americanjewisharchives.org/ms/ms0882/00409/ms0882.00409.2001-04-01.pdf) is publicly indexed there, but its content and local advertisements identify a **New Jersey Temple Emanuel** near Cherry Hill and Voorhees, not Tucson Temple Emanu-El. This provides an additional **concrete rejected name-match**, not evidence that the missing 2001 Tucson sermon is preserved in MS-882. Neither the catalogue page nor the specific indexed bulletin confirms a Tucson 2000, 2001 or 2015 issue.
