## NEW: original HIAS-hosted December 2015 multi-rabbi signatory document

The [primary HIAS PDF, *1000 Rabbis in Support of Welcoming Refugees*](https://hias.org/wp-content/uploads/hias_1000_rabbis_support_refugees.pdf) is an **original organization-hosted copy of a separate public advocacy document** dated **December 2, 2015**. Its signatory appendix says **1,216 rabbis as of 1 p.m. on December 4**. The publicly parsed document explicitly names **Rabbi Samuel M. Cohon — Tucson, AZ** in its alphabetized C-section.

[Contemporaneous KOLD Tucson News Now reporting on December 4, 2015](https://www.kold.com/story/30672691/tucson-rabbi-signs-national-letter-in-support-of-welcoming-refugees/) independently identifies Rabbi Cohon, then at Temple Emanu-El, as a signer and quotes him about the refugees. This confirms that the signatory listing is not just an ambiguous later-name match.

**Critical classification:** The HIAS document is a collectively **signed** statement. It is **not** Rabbi Cohon's individually authored or delivered *The Necessary Chutzpah of Saving Refugees*, which is known only from [the contemporaneous URJ sermon excerpts](https://urj.org/blog/our-second-annual-rosh-hashanah-sermon-round) and an inaccessible original Temple sermon path. Signing a December letter does not establish that the September sermon text is preserved with HIAS, that its words were reused, or that HIAS owns an original sermon manuscript. No new individually authored work, missing original, audio file or core/supplemental publication record has been created from this evidence.

**PDF QA limits:** The source text parser supplied the document's date and an alphabetized list including Cohon. A required PDF screenshot on page index 2 returned a cache miss, so image-level visual confirmation, original PDF bytes, checksum and signature authenticity verification were not performed. The figure 1,216 comes from the PDF's own stated as-of header; it is not an independently recomputed count.
