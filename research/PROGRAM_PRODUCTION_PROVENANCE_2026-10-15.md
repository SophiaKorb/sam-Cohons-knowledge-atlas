# Too Jewish production provenance — 2026-10-15

## New first-party and contemporaneous source triangulation

1. **Program ownership and original launch:** The U.S. trademark record for TOO JEWISH, serial 76521880, registration 2961720, identifies **Samuel M. Cohon** as registrant; reports first use August 4, 2002 and first use in commerce August 19, 2002; describes an educational and entertainment religious talk-variety radio program series. This is trademark and program-identity evidence **not proof of copyright ownership in individual recordings, present possession of masters, or permission to redistribute**. Source: https://trademarks.justia.com/765/21/too-76521880.html
2. **Program production/format in 2005:** Contemporary reporting describes the Tucson-based production and regular “and Friends” commentators including a former diplomat, an Israeli reporter and Amy Hirshberg Lederman. It identifies KXAM 1310 Phoenix Sunday-morning carriage. This identifies participants and distribution practices but **not an archivist, recording engineer, master file location, or retention policy**. Source: https://www.phoenixnewtimes.com/arts-culture/jew-talk-too-much-6397604/
3. **Podcast date boundary:** Moment's article *Top Ten Jewish Podcasts* states that the program had aired since 2002 but **introduced its podcast version in 2010**. This materially explains why early 2004–2009 broadcasts may have no original podcast catalogue entries; it does **not** prove that pre-2010 broadcasts were never retrospectively uploaded. Source: https://momentmag.com/top-ten-jewish-podcasts/
4. **Current distribution:** Official Podbean description confirms continuing Arizona broadcast via KVOI 1030, KAPR 930 and KJAA 1240, with podcast episodes separately presented. Source: https://toojewishradio.podbean.com/p/about/
5. **Program's own written description:** A 2023 guest profile attributes production to Tucson and notes recurring mavens Tom Price and Guy Gelbart, adding possible production-history interview leads but no established masters. Source: https://www.wesaidgotravel.com/brave-ish-on-too-jewish-radio/
6. **Program archive:** Official indexed broadcast roster spans early dates with individually formatted MP3 paths. Catalog listing is evidence of historical broadcast metadata, not present availability of recorded audio. Source: https://www.toojewishradio.com/too_jewish_shows.htm

## Custodian priority based on this evidence

- **First priority: program-originated production records.** Investigate whether original program masters and pre-podcast MP3s were retained by the Too Jewish producer. The trademark record establishes Samuel M. Cohon's connection to the program but does not settle audio ownership, media possession, or rights.
- **Second priority: historical KVOI operations.** Ask about syndicated program-delivery format (live feed, CD-R, digital file, or off-air capture), 690-to-1030 move and any retained logs.
- **Third priority: KXAM station personnel/ownership.** Establish whether KXAM recorded syndicated programs before 2009 closure, rather than assuming it kept copies.
- **Fourth priority: guest- or institutional-held copies.** Yale's 2004 Evan Wolfson CD-R is an independently documented holding, not a newly recovered digital master.

## Distinct gates

**Investigated:** production identity, trademark chronology, period carriage and podcast boundary. **Not verified:** specific producer/engineer, production workflow, storage arrangements, custody of any original master, 2004–2009 playable files. **Not done:** direct inquiry, copy request, transcript authentication, rights clearance, editorial approval or deployment.
