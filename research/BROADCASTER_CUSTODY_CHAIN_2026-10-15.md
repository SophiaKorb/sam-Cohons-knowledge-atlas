# Too Jewish broadcaster custody chain — 2026-10-15

**Purpose:** Identify evidence-based custodial contacts for 2004–2009 Too Jewish masters. All names below refer to historical station operation; none is a verified holder of surviving tapes or files.

## KVOI, Tucson

- Contemporary *Tucson Weekly* (Aug 2004) documents the program's carriage on **KVOI 690 AM**: https://www.tucsonweekly.com/collections/aug-5-11-2004/
- Historical FCC database filing **20040218AMX**, facility **24590**, identifies **Good News Broadcasting, Inc.** as KVOI 690 licensee and **Douglas E. Martin** as president: https://www.fcc.report/CDBS/980638
- FCC **20050601CDU** corroborates the Good News entity and historical address/phone: https://www.fcc.report/CDBS/1047685
- FCC **20080211AGP** continues to identify Good News and Douglas E. Martin in 2008: https://www.fcc.report/CDBS/1235320
- *Tucson Weekly* (July 2009) documents the **July 1, 2009** frequency/call-sign swap: KVOI programming moved from AM 690 to AM 1030; the former 690 became KCEE: https://www.tucsonweekly.com/newsopinion/media-watch-1260014/
- Current station contact: https://www.kvoi.com/contact-us. This is a routing lead, **not evidence the present owner possesses old program audio**.
- A later 2018 transfer to Bustos Media means historical licensee and current owner must not be conflated.

**Custody questions:** Did Good News/KVOI make or retain off-air recordings of syndicated Sunday programs? Was Too Jewish delivered as tape/CD, digital download, FTP, or live feed? Who handled producer archives during the 2009 frequency move and 2018 sale? Were audio hard drives, program logs or backups transferred?

## KXAM 1310, Phoenix/Mesa

- March 17, 2005 *Phoenix New Times* establishes Too Jewish carriage: https://www.phoenixnewtimes.com/arts-culture/jew-talk-too-much-6397604/
- Contemporary April 2009 *Arizona Republic* report (preserved by radio-hobby publication) attributes the shutdown decision to the **Gerson family**, and names **Don Sandler** as station manager. It mentions recordings of a different host's programs, illustrating that some taped-program workflow existed, but **not** evidence that Too Jewish recordings were preserved: https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-DX/IRCA-DXM/DXM-Vol-46/DXM_Vol_46_No_27-2.pdf
- Broadcast historian Scott Fybush independently describes April 2009 shutdown and subsequent new ownership as KIHP: https://www.fybush.com/sites/2010/site-100723.html
- Historical station-owner name **Embree Broadcasting** appears in retrospective station histories; confirm exact licensee and transfers against FCC facility records before treating its personnel as authoritative custodian contacts.
- National Radio Club bulletin documents sale of the silent KXAM operation to Immaculate Heart Radio in 2009: https://nationalradioclub.org/DXNs/V76/DXN76_29.pdf

**Custody questions:** Were syndicated-show audio archives maintained by KXAM staff, the Gerson family/Embree, or outside production? Was program storage retained when KXAM ceased operations or passed to the successor licensee? Does the old program supplier have delivery archives?

## Separation of evidence and approval

- **Verified:** station carriage, FCC historical ownership for KVOI, 2009 frequency swap and historical KXAM transition.
- **Unverified:** possession or survival of original Too Jewish masters, whether stations even recorded every broadcast, present custodianship, digital file access.
- **Not attempted/obtained:** direct contact, acquisition, rights clearance, editorial approval, publication authorization or site deployment.
